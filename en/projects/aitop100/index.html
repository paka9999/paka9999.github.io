The competition

Participants used AI agents to solve assigned missions. I competed individually in the November 21, 2026 final at Kakao AI Campus. During hands-off rounds, participants could neither give instructions nor change code, so the agents had to decide what to do and act on their own.

Dividing execution and log analysis

I assigned mission execution to the primary agent and log analysis to supporting agents in Seongju Studio. The primary agent used their analysis to decide what to do next.

Running the final 90 minutes

I wrote down the current state, objectives, and remaining tasks in a handoff file so the agent could continue without the earlier chat. The agent read the file and carried out missions for the final 90 minutes without further instructions.
