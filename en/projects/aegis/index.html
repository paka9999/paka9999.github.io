My role

AEGIS is an AI security gateway that inspects requests and responses sent to external AI services. I fixed issues found during GS certification and worked on access checks, auditing, Korean personal-data detection and the operations console for a financial-sector rollout.

Access checks and audit logging

I moved administrative authorization into a shared check and added regression tests for the actions each role can take. I added audit records so operators could review changes to accounts, permissions and administrative settings.

Personal-data detection and policy notices

I improved detection and masking of personal data in Korean text and reduced unnecessary masking of routine work requests. In AI chat clients that showed only a generic error for a blocked request, I changed the response so the policy notice appeared in the conversation.

Operations and verification

I added filters for risk type, severity and AI service, and fixed date-range queries in the decision log. I built organization management and policy backup and restore screens, and documented the pre-installation checks and operating procedures.

Summary

I fixed issues found in AEGIS certification testing in June 2026. During the August and September financial-sector rollout, I repaired access control and auditing and built screens for decision log filters, organization sync and policy backups.

  • Added a shared authorization check to write routes with missing role checks and covered it with regression tests
  • Added audit logs for changes to credentials, roles, admin settings, LLM keys and data rights requests
  • Improved detection of Korean personal data and reduced unnecessary masking of routine work text
  • Screens and data: judgment-log filters, end-date-off-by-one and UTC display fixes, organization management (NAC sync), policy backup/restore
