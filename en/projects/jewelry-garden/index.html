Jewelry GardenJJJ STUDIO

Tap a gem to change it and its neighbors. Match every tile to the target gem in this portrait puzzle game.

Role
Puzzle design, Unity implementation, audio and effects, device QA
Period
Nov 2026
Status
Windows and Android testing
Stack

Building and solving a puzzle

Holding a gem previews which tiles will change. Releasing it commits the move. I added cancel, undo, restart and hints. The generator builds solvable boards and sets move limits using the shortest solution.

Winter workshop home and garden map

The home menu uses six enamel buttons of the same size. Continue opens the last unlocked garden. The map shows whether the selected garden is locked and displays the best score. I checked the home and map in both a narrow portrait view and a simulated wider app window.

Find and equip collected jewels

The full-screen jewel box keeps the three equip slots and starter gems in view. Players can search their collection below by Korean or English name and by color. In regular gardens, players pick all three gems individually. Choosing one already in use swaps the slots. Players can equip a reward from the clear screen or arrange it in the display case. I tested search and equipping on a physical device and checked that the equipped jewels persisted after a restart.

Winter workshop audio and effects

For the early build, I made button and gem sounds in ChipTone and edited the workshop and seasonal music in LMMS. Later, I used tracks made in Music Workshop as background music and brought sound effects made with the tool into the game.

I used Effekseer for gem glints and star effects. I added separate master, music and effects controls and an in-puzzle music toggle. The device capture shows the controls and the music toggle during a puzzle.

Rankings based on validated solutions

Players get the same puzzle on a given date. Cloud Code validates the proof of solution and checks the submitted time range. The daily leaderboard sorts by move count first and solve time second. Weekly rankings count the best score for each date once. I completed and submitted a puzzle on a physical device and checked its daily and weekly UGS entries against the device result. Regular gardens save hint and undo allowances for each puzzle.

Summary

I built a one-handed Unity puzzle with a winter workshop home and garden map, a solvable board generator, a searchable jewel box with three equip slots, daily rankings and workshop audio.

  • Shows affected tiles while the player holds a gem and commits on release
  • Generates solvable gardens and shared daily puzzles, with UGS leaderboards
  • Searches jewels by name and color, equips three gems, and saves the display case layout
