Game prototypes

  • Jewelry Garden

    A portrait puzzle where tapping a gem changes neighboring colors. The winter workshop includes daily puzzles and a jewel box with name and color filters and three equip slots.

    I built the shortest-solution-based puzzle generator, touch controls and server-validated daily and weekly rankings. I also added music and sound effects made in Music Workshop.

    Gameplay and development

  • SproutLab

    A gardening game where plants keep growing in real time while the app is closed.

    I built continuous plant growth, condition-based care guidance and persistent pot placement. I adapted layouts to foldable window sizes and checked installation on a foldable device.

  • Free Casino

    A mobile prototype with several table games played using virtual chips.

    I implemented and tested craps and four-spot keno. In development, I added a UGS backend for atomic transactions and connected backups for accounts signed in with Google.

  • LastLift

    A portrait puzzle about moving passengers with one elevator before the timer runs out.

  • BeatSpark

    A team platformer built around timing jumps and dodging obstacles.

  • OneShotDungeon

    A ricochet puzzle where one shot bounces off walls and mirrors to hit every enemy in a room.

  • Glide

    A puzzle about sliding across ice until an obstacle stops you.
